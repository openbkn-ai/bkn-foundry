Grants registered operations to custom roles. Available only when the Enterprise rbac_basic capability is assembled and licensed.

Role permissions

Grant operations to a custom role

Requires admin-role:permissions; the legacy admin-authz:grant permission is also accepted during the deprecation window. Built-in roles cannot be modified through this endpoint.

Resource types and operations must be registered. Wildcard resource types and operations are rejected. Resource identifiers containing * are rejected for registered sub-resource types (types with a parent), including partial patterns such as prefix-* and parent/*. Permitted top-level type-wide grants retain their existing behavior. Other guards, including the prohibition on type-wide action execution, still apply.

A rejected request preserves existing permissions and creates no partial grants or business audit event. Registry and database failures remain HTTP 500 and do not expose internal exception details.
